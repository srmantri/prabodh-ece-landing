About the ECE Course

Everything you need to start a rewarding career as an early childhood educator.

🗓️ Duration & Format

6-month comprehensive program. Choose from Online, Offline (Pune) or Hybrid batches to suit your schedule.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • Child Development & Understanding Children
  • Curriculum Planning
  • Pre-school Administration
  • Teaching Aids & Digital Classroom Tools

🧩 Practical Training

  • Hands-on activities & classroom application
  • Internships across school boards & special needs facilities
  • Field visits to preschools
  • Workshops, seminars & teaching-aid exhibitions

🎓 Board Exposure & Alignment

Curriculum aligned with NEP 2020, with exposure to SSC, CBSE, ICSE & IB board philosophies — preparing you for schools of every kind.

💼 Placement Support
